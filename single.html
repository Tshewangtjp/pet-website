Golden Retriever

Age: 3 Months Breed: Golden Retriever Price: ₹750

The Golden Retriever is known for its friendly and tolerant attitude. This breed is incredibly intelligent and eager to please, making them a perfect companion for families and first-time pet owners.

Golden Retrievers are highly trainable and enjoy activities such as fetching, swimming, and obedience training. They thrive on social interaction and love spending time with their humans.

"Loyal, playful, and gentle, Golden Retrievers are the ideal family pet for those seeking a loving and dependable companion."

Taking care of a Golden Retriever requires regular exercise and grooming. Their thick double coat needs brushing several times a week to keep them looking their best. With proper care, these dogs can live up to 12 years or more.
